# Whiskey Salmon Marinade
*Transcriber: uniquegeek*

## Ingredients
| | |
| --- | --- |
| ½ cup	| Soy sauce |
| ½ cup	| Whiskey |
| ¼ cup	| Vegetable oil |
| 1 tbsp | Fresh ginger, minced |
| 4 cloves | Fresh garlic, minced |
| 1 tsp	| Black pepper, ground |
| 1-2 tsp	| Sesame oil |
| ¼ cup	| Demerara sugar (dark brown sugar) |
| | (or white sugar + 1 tbsp warmed molassess) |
| 1 tbsp | Sesame seeds, garnish (optional) |

## Marinade:
- Mix ingredients, marinade salmon 2-12 hours.

## Cooking Options:
Reserve marinade separately for sauce, below:

	### BBQ:
	1. Barbecue salmon until done (thickest part is just flaky).

	### Oven:
	1. Line an oven-safe dish with parchment. Place salmon skin-side down.
	2. Pour a couple tablespoons of marinade over salmon.
	3. Bake in an oven at 350F for about 25 minutes, until done.

## Sauce:
1. While salmon is cooking, mix 1 tbsp cornstarch with small amount of marinade in a small dish.
2. Mix in with rest of marinade in a small saucepan.
3. Bring marinade to a boil, stirring frequently. Let boil 3 minutes.

Garnish with sesame seeds, if desired. Serve sauce separately with salmon.
