# Mocha Cake
*Transciber: uniquegeek*

## Mocha sauce:
| ½ cup	Cocoa powder |
| 1 cup	Sugar |
| 1 cup	Brown sugar |
| 6 tbsp	flour |
| 4 cups	Hot coffee |

1. In a saucepan, mix together dry ingredients.
2. Add small amount of cooled coffee to dry ingredients to make a thin paste.
3. Slowly mix in rest of coffee.
4. Bring to a boil, stirring occasionally.
5. Turn down heat, stir occasionally until sugar is fully incorporated (a few minutes).
6. Remove sauce from heat.

## Cake:
1/3 cup	shortening
2/3 cup	White sugar
1	egg
½ tsp	Vanilla extract
1 cup	flour
1 ½ tsp	Baking powder
¼ tsp	Salt
1/3 cup	milk

1. With a mixer, cream butter and sugar until light and fluffy.
2. Add egg and vanilla, beat in until incorporated.
3. Mix dry ingredients together, sift flour if you’re feeling fancy.
4. With a wooden spoon, mix all ingredients together. (Are you supposed to scald the milk first? Who knows.)
5. Bake at 350F for 35-40 minutes.
6. Let cake cool, add mocha sauce on top.
